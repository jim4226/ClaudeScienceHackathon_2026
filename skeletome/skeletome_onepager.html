The gap nobody noticed

Every experiment asking what makes the human genome human was run in brain cells.

Human Accelerated Regions are the fastest-evolving patches of our genome — mostly regulatory switches. Every published HAR reporter assay (Pollard, Uebbing, Whalen) measured them in neural cells. So we asked the skeleton instead — the tissue behind height, hips, and osteoarthritis — and found the data cupboard bare.

That emptiness is the whole opportunity: the missing skeletal HAR assay doesn't exist in a dish, so we build it in silico — with the honest register of a screen, not a claim of measured function.

The method, in one base-flip

Three gates decide whether a mutation is signal.

For each human-specific base inside a HAR, we ask three independent questions. Only substitutions that pass all three — deeply conserved, not a gene-conversion artifact, and moving skeletal accessibility — become candidates.

01 · Deep constraint

241-mammal Zoonomia phyloP over the exact substitution positions. A base mammals held still for 100 million years, then flipped on the human lineage, is interesting. phyloP>2.27 (5% FDR).

02 · gBGC control

19–33% of HARs are GC-biased gene conversion, not selection. We classify every base W→S / S→W, join local recombination rate, and match the null on it — turning the field's favorite objection into our headline filter.

03 · Skeletal effect

Ancestral vs derived allele scored through AlphaGenome + ENCODE ChromBPNet limb / MSC / osteoblast models, with a neural comparator so "skeletal-specific" actually means specific.

The build — 6 phases, GPU-free critical path

A pipeline a two-person team ships in five days.

AlphaGenome (hosted API, no GPU) is the primary engine and guarantees the full result alone. Base-resolution ChromBPNet is an enrichment layer, gated behind a two-hour hour-1 smoke test — if the stack fights back, we drop it and still ship.

Where it could die — and the fix

Every kill-shot has a same-week answer.

Kill-shot

gBGC confound. A fifth to a third of HARs are gene conversion, not selection — the reviewer's first question.

Mitigation

Make gBGC a first-class headline arm: W→S classification, recombination-matched null. "Most HARs are noise — here are the few that aren't."

Kill-shot

ChromBPNet / GPU hell burns days on version mismatches.

Mitigation

AlphaGenome (GPU-free) is primary and ships alone. ChromBPNet gated behind a hard 2-hour test — fail fast, stop fighting the stack.

Kill-shot

GDF5 circularity. Tune until it surfaces, then announce it surfaced — and n=1 proves nothing.

Mitigation

Freeze thresholds + direction before looking. Control set (GROW1, R4, HACNS1) + negative controls. Report genuine precision/recall.

Kill-shot

"In-silico MPRA" over-claims a functional readout.

Mitigation

Rename to variant-effect screen. Methods state: predicted accessibility deltas, not measured transcription. Honest register throughout.
