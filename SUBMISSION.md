## Submission description

HumanVector asks what biological-age models erase when they compress a complex
system into one number. Four phase-specific ECG clocks are represented as a
shared movement, an unsigned disagreement magnitude, and a signed physiological
direction. The prespecified mortality test for disagreement was null. A
randomized IKr-blocker experiment then revealed a stable repolarization
direction that a second IKr-active drug supported and that was carried frozen
into an external QT-extension phenotype test. Blood biomarkers, brain MRI,
multi-region CT, movement, and evolutionary genomics provide a supporting
cross-scale atlas with explicit evidence labels and cohort boundaries. The
release includes the paper, code, frozen model artifacts, aggregate results,
claim-to-artifact ledger, public interactive atlas, and fresh-clone verification
commands.
