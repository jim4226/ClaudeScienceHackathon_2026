## Bundled PTB-XL ECG examples

Three unmodified 500 Hz, 10-second, 12-lead PTB-XL records are bundled in the
demo under CC BY 4.0:

- `00176_hr` (`ecg_id` 176)
- `00233_hr` (`ecg_id` 233)
- `00420_hr` (`ecg_id` 420)

Source: [PTB-XL v1.0.3 on PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/content/ptb-xl/1.0.3/).

Please cite Wagner P, Strodthoff N, Bousseljot RD, et al. *PTB-XL, a large
publicly available electrocardiography dataset*. Scientific Data. 2020;7:154,
and the standard PhysioNet citation by Goldberger et al.

The exact record manifest and attribution are in
`electrical-body-clock/demo/hf_space/examples/`.

## Other scientific sources

Aggregate or derived outputs use CODE-15%, SaMi-Trop, Chapman-Shaoxing/Ningbo,
ECGRDVQ, STAFF III, EchoNext, NHANES, AABC/HCP-Aging, MPI LEMON,
TotalSegmentator, and public comparative-genomics resources. Their access
classes, source links, redistribution boundaries, and required acknowledgments
are recorded in `DATA_LICENSES.md` and the manuscript.

No AABC or EchoNext participant-level data are included in the public release.
